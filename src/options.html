File names

Click a field to add it. Anything in < > is left out when it's empty, and / makes a folder.

A folder inside Downloads, like Libby. Leave it blank to save straight into Downloads.

Downloading

Libby blocks downloads for a while if you take too many too quickly. 0 turns the pause off. Firefox pauses twice as often.

Buttons in Libby

Separate buttons are combined into one when there's no room for all three, like while you have a book open in Libby.

Progress panel

0 keeps it open. It stays open while your pointer is over it, while you're in another tab or window, and if something went wrong.

Troubleshooting

The log of your last run, to paste into a bug report. It's there even after the panel has closed. It lists problems first, with the steps that led to each one.
